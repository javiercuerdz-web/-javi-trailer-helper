JAVI TRAILER HELPER V6 - ONE-TIME AI SETUP

The app works immediately with the improved Quick Scan and full VIN support.
The Analyze with AI button requires a private backend so your OpenAI key is never exposed in GitHub Pages.

Backend files included:
- cloudflare-worker.js
- wrangler.toml.example

Setup outline:
1. Create a Cloudflare account and a Worker.
2. Paste cloudflare-worker.js into the Worker.
3. Add a secret named OPENAI_API_KEY in the Worker settings.
4. Deploy and copy the workers.dev URL.
5. In the app tap the three-dot menu, paste that URL under AI Photo Analysis, save, and test.

Do not put your OpenAI API key in index.html, app.js, GitHub, or the app settings.
